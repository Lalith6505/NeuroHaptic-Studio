Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026
Application Name: NeuroHaptic Studio: Haptic Synth
Package Name: com.neurohaptic.studio

1. Overview & Zero-Cloud Privacy Commitment

NeuroHaptic Studio is an offline developer and sound design utility built for haptic synthesis and tactile waveform generation. We maintain an absolute zero-cloud architecture: we do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or monetize any personal user data, device identifiers, haptic designs, or analytics telemetry.

2. Hardware & Device Permissions

To deliver tactile sensations, NeuroHaptic Studio requests access to device hardware:

  • Vibration Permission (android.permission.VIBRATE): Required exclusively to drive the device's physical Linear Resonant Actuator (LRA) or vibration motor in response to user waveform parameters and preset auditions.
  • Screen Wake Lock (android.permission.WAKE_LOCK): Used solely to prevent the screen from dimming while continuous haptic calibration or spatial testing is active.

3. Zero Third-Party Trackers & Zero Advertisements

NeuroHaptic Studio contains no advertising SDKs, no behavioral trackers, and no external analytics frameworks (no Google Firebase Analytics, no Facebook SDK, etc.). The application operates 100% locally on your device CPU.

4. Code Generation & Intellectual Property

Any haptic waveforms, presets, or source code snippets (Kotlin, C++, C#) generated by NeuroHaptic Studio belong 100% to you. We claim no ownership, royalties, or licensing restrictions over your exported tactile assets.

5. Children's Privacy

NeuroHaptic Studio does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. No accounts, sign-ins, or emails are ever requested.

6. Contact Information

For technical support or inquiries, please contact our development team via our GitHub repository issues portal or the Google Play developer contact page.
